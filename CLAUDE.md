- Table row headers: use "AI Features" (not "AI Insights")

- NEVER describe Zella AI as just "pattern detection" or "analytics engine" — always lead with the 3 agents

## What Zella AI Is
- **AI trading partner** (NOT a trading bot, does NOT execute trades)
- **3 autonomous agents** that work in the background without prompting:
  1. **Session Review** — automatically journals your entire trading day, flags rule breaks, delivers structured recap
  2. **Auto Trade Tagger** — categorizes every trade based on rules you define
  3. **Market Sentiment Briefing** — generates a personalized pre-market plan each morning
- **Conversational AI** — ask questions about your data in plain language, get instant data-grounded answers
- **AI-powered pattern detection** — scans complete trading history across 50+ reports
- Zella AI is LAUNCHED (not coming soon) — launched before June 2026
- Scenario Replay is COMING SOON (not yet launched)
- Prop Firm Sync is also LAUNCHED
